- `scripts/compile-snippets.sh` without `JAVA_HOME`, on macOS, takes the newest JDK `java_home` lists that is 17 or newer and not GraalVM, and names the JDKs it skipped and the one it took — the order `gradle-toolchain` already gives. Before, `java` on `PATH` ran the newest JDK, so a machine with GraalVM beside an OpenJDK had every snippet refused. An explicit `JAVA_HOME` is taken as given
