- a new agent-facing skill, `task-documents`, holds what a task's `Task.md`, `Research.md` and `Plan.md` carry: three layers — what and why, how it works today, how to do it — the rules every document follows, and a worked example; `task-new`, every investigating stage, its panel lenses and every Plan stage of both dispatch forms point at its sections rather than restating them
- a step's `Task.md` template carries three anchors inside `## 3. [Task]`: `### Expected behaviour`, `### Questions for Research`, `### Acceptance`; an anchor that does not apply carries `— <reason>`, and `task-new` verifies all three when it creates a step
- `scripts/lint-artifact-budget.sh --task-docs` measures the `Task.md` of every step not yet started, at any scale, against a ceiling of 100 lines and for its three anchors; the orchestrator runs it after an epic's Plan — an `auto` Method A range is split at Plan so the measurement lands before any step runs — and sends a step back to the architect once
- a project may move any ceiling in a new optional `## Budgets` block of `CLAUDE-spine-toolkit.md`; the lint is its one reader, `--budgets` prints the resolved map, and the Outbound Contract ships it as the new `budgets` field, which an epic also hands to the steps it pushes
- steps written before this release carry no anchors and stay quiet: the lint reads a step's `Task.md` only with `--task-docs`, the orchestrator passes it only after an epic's Plan, and a started or closed step is never measured — re-planning an old epic sends only its not-yet-started steps back for anchors
- the reference driver floor moves to `>=1.10.0 <2` with this release; nothing the platforms vendor moved, so none of them has to re-vendor
