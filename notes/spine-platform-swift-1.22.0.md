- `swift-validator` treats a direct test run that `long-run.sh wait` reports `stalled` after its summary line, with a `simctl diagnose` in its process tree, as finished: it ends the run with `long-run.sh stop`, takes the verdict from the summary lines and reports it as passed or failed, not as hung, without rerunning the tests. `swift-tester` and `swift-diagnostics` point at that rule. A `test_sim` call is not covered, because it blocks the agent until `xcodebuild` exits; the flag is its only protection
- the reason for `-collect-test-diagnostics never` is corrected: `xcodebuild` collects diagnostics after a green run too, not only after a failure. Measured on Xcode 27.0: without the flag a green run of 2488 tests sat silent for more than eight minutes on a hung `simctl diagnose`; with it the process exited 46 s after the summary line
- the `spine-toolkit` floor moves to `>=2.8.0 <3`: the fallback relies on `long-run.sh` and its `stalled` status
