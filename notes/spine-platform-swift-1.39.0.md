- `swift-validator` classifies a failing test by spine-toolkit's `### Failures outside the task` and reads the project's known failures registry from the brief's `known_failures`, never writing it: a known failure outside the task leaves `PASSED`, an unregistered one green on its retry gives `PASSED_WITH_FLAKES` with `continue`, a failure inside the task is `FAILED` whatever the registry says. `FLAKY` stays with a TEST task's own added tests. It writes `## Outside the task` and returns `outside_failures` and `known_failures_cleared`
- a re-run, and a first Validation, carry a module from a phase's `ran:` line or a green row of an earlier Validation; a null `rerun` runs what no carried run covers rather than the whole set. A `reach` line of the lint is left for Review
- requires spine-toolkit 2.44.0
