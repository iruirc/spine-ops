- a package's `## Public API` reads its real target folders: `Sources/<package>` as before when it exists, otherwise every folder under `Sources/` — one gives the flat list, several give a `### <target>` group each. A package whose target is named unlike its folder (`myapp-core` with `Sources/Core/`) no longer regenerates to `- none`. Hidden folders are skipped and a symlink to a folder counts; `Package.swift` is not read, so a target with a custom `path:` outside `Sources/` is still not listed
